# Daily Wisdom

A curated pool the app samples once a day for the "daily gem." Mark loves the Stoics and Marcus Aurelius, so the set leans there, with a few lines from the coach's other voices. Attribution is kept honest: well-sourced quotes are stated plainly, popular paraphrases are flagged so the app never presents a shaky attribution as gospel.

## Marcus Aurelius (Meditations)

- "The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way." (5.20)
- "Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one." (10.16)
- "You could leave life right now. Let that determine what you do and say and think." (2.11)
- "If it is not right, do not do it; if it is not true, do not say it." (12.17)
- The morning prep (2.1, faithful short form): "When you wake, tell yourself you'll meet interference, ingratitude, and people who get in the way. None of it can touch what's yours to control. Begin anyway."
- "Confine yourself to the present."

## Seneca

- "We suffer more often in imagination than in reality." (Letters)
- "It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste much of it." (On the Shortness of Life)
- "Difficulties strengthen the mind, as labor does the body."
- "Begin at once to live, and count each separate day as a separate life." (Letters)

## Epictetus

- "It's not what happens to you, but how you react that matters."
- "No man is free who is not master of himself."
- "First say to yourself what you would be, then do what you have to do." (Discourses)

## Scott Adams

- "Goals are for losers. Systems are for winners."
- "Maximize your personal energy, not the number of tasks."
- "A reframe doesn't have to be true. It has to be useful."

## In the spirit of Hormozi (paraphrased standards, not verbatim quotes)

- "The work works. Your only job is to keep showing up to it."
- "You're not behind. You're early in a long game."
- "Nobody is coming to save you. That's good news. It means it's yours."

## How the app uses this

- One gem per day, rotated so the same line doesn't repeat for a couple of weeks.
- Match the gem to the active campaign when possible. During the sleep-shift, favor lines on discipline, the present, and doing the work anyway.
- Show the source. If a line is a popular paraphrase rather than a sourced quote, label it that way. Mark hates getting things wrong, and a fake Aurelius quote is exactly the kind of mistake to avoid.
- The coach can reference the day's gem in the evening reflection if it fits, but never forces it.
