# Mind System Playbook

Adams' "imagination" driver. Picture an incredible future, and reframe the negative before it drains you. This is the head game that makes the physical systems stick. Keep it light and practical, Stoic, not woo. Mark wants this present but not heavy.

The app delivers this as the Mind detail page: the pinned vision, an optional morning intention, the evening reflection, today's gem, and the reframe library. Same clean design as the other playbooks.

## The idea

Two moves. Hold a clear picture of where you're going so daily actions have a direction, and catch negative self-talk and swap it for a frame that moves you. Adams: a reframe doesn't have to be true, it has to be useful. The Stoics add the daily practice: control what you can, expect friction, treat the obstacle as the path.

## Components

**Future vision (pinned, editable).** Your stated direction: leader with your own team, multi-entrepreneur with several income streams, athletic, free, strong, focused. Pinned at the top so it's visible, not a daily essay. Edit it whenever it sharpens. The coach reconnects your daily actions to it now and then, not every day.

**Morning intention (light, optional).** One line to set the day's posture, Aurelius-style: expect friction, stay locked in, work the system. Optional, ten seconds. You said this might be too much some days, so it's never forced.

**Evening reflection.** Already in the check-in. This playbook frames the prompt: what moved you forward, what pulled you off, what's the one adjustment. Short.

**Daily gem.** One quote a day from the wisdom pool (coach-knowledge/daily-wisdom.md), Stoic-leaning, rotated so it doesn't repeat for a couple of weeks, attribution shown honestly. A small hit of perspective, not a lecture.

**Reframe library.** Browseable from coach-knowledge/reframes-library.md. The real value: when your check-in words go negative ("I'm lazy," "I'm behind"), the coach catches it and offers a reframe, old frame to new frame, with the cue to repeat it. You can also pin favorites.

## What the app runs

- Shows your pinned vision, today's gem, an optional one-line morning intention, and a button into the reframe library.
- The check-in logs the morning intention (if set) and the evening reflection (already there).
- The coach surfaces a reframe when it detects negative self-talk, can tie the day back to the vision, and may reference the gem in the evening read if it fits. It never forces any of it.

## Keep it light

This system fails if it gets heavy or preachy. One line, one gem, one reframe when needed. That's the whole footprint on a normal day.
