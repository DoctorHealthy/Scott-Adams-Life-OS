# Morning & Schedule System Playbook

Adams' "flexible schedule" driver. Control over your time is one of the biggest energy levers, and for you it's contested: work owns roughly 3pm to 10pm and it's different every week. The move is to protect the hours you do control (before 3pm), and stop fighting the hours you don't.

The app delivers this as the Morning & schedule detail page: the protected morning block, the slot-when-free list, your fixed rocks, and the personal Eisenhower matrix. Same clean design as the other playbooks.

## The idea

Your mornings before 3pm are the prize. That's where training, the park, deep personal work, and real energy live, and right now the late sleep cycle is eating them. As the sleep system pulls your wake time earlier, this system fills those mornings with the right things and guards them. The unpredictable work window gets a different tool: not rigid blocks, but a list you pick from whenever a gap opens.

## Components

**The protected morning block.** A flexible sequence for your pre-3pm hours, tied to the sleep campaign's morning routine:

- Morning light within 30 to 60 minutes of waking (also your sleep anchor and your nature time).
- Ondra warm-up (from the Exercise system).
- A 90-minute deep or personal work block while energy is fresh.
- Training session.
- Park or reading if time allows.

This is the default shape, not a rigid clock. The point is the morning isn't wasted.

**Slot-when-free list (the 3pm to 10pm window).** Your work hours are random week to week, so don't schedule personal tasks into them. Instead, keep a short list of personal and venture tasks you can grab in any gap, between meetings, during a lull. Pull, don't plan.

**Fixed rocks.** German on Tuesday and Friday. Anything else that's genuinely fixed. The app knows these so the morning plan works around them.

**Personal Eisenhower matrix (life and ventures only, no work).** Four quadrants for your personal projects and new income-stream ideas. Work tasks and ClickUp stay out, this is the personal OS.

- Q1 Important + Urgent: do now.
- Q2 Important + Not urgent: protect this, it's where growth lives. The system defends Q2 time.
- Q3 Not important + Urgent: delegate or batch.
- Q4 Not important + Not urgent: cut.

The matrix sorts in code. The coach flags when Q2 is getting crowded out and pushes Q3 and Q4 toward delegation or elimination.

## What the app runs

- Shows the morning block plan, the slot-when-free list, the fixed rocks, and the Eisenhower matrix.
- Add, edit, and move items between quadrants. Q1 to Q4 sorting and any counts are computed in code.
- The check-in logs whether the morning block held.
- The coach protects the morning block, ties a protected morning to higher energy using your real data, flags a crowded Q2, and nudges Q3 and Q4 items toward delegation or the bin.
