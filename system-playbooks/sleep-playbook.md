# Sleep System Playbook

The keystone. Fix this and the mornings you want (training, the park, an early work block, real energy) open up. Everything else rides on it. Built on Adams' systems thinking plus Huberman's verified protocols.

The app delivers this as the Sleep detail page: your current step, tonight's target bedtime, the wind-down checklist, and the morning-light reminder. All step and consistency math is in code.

## The idea

You're at bed 2 to 3am, wake 10 to 11am, and tired. You can't jump to an 8am wake, the body clock doesn't move that fast. So we shift it in small steps and let the wake time lead. Huberman's rule: a consistent wake time matters more than a consistent bedtime, and morning light is the strongest lever you have. We use both.

## The metric

Two things only, at first: wake-time consistency (the lead metric) and bedtime. These feed the master metric, energy. The app tracks how close your actual wake time is to your target, every day.

## The shift engine (the system)

- Set a target wake time slightly earlier than your current real one. Not your dream time, the next step.
- Move the target earlier by 15 to 30 minutes only after the current step holds for several days in a row.
- Wake time leads. You hold the wake time even after a bad night, and bedtime drifts earlier on its own because you'll be tired earlier.
- Example path: 10:30, then 10:00, then 9:30, stepping down to your 8:00 to 8:30 goal over a couple of months. Slow is the point.

The app shows your current step and advances it when you've been consistent. The coach pushes the next step and calls out drift.

## Morning light (your strongest lever)

Within 30 to 60 minutes of waking, get outside light: 5 to 10 minutes on a clear day, 15 to 20 if it's overcast. No sunglasses. This anchors your clock and pulls your whole rhythm earlier. It also doubles as the nature and park time that recharges you, so it's two wins in one. This is non-negotiable in the protocol.

## Wind-down (anchored to reading)

Brushing teeth does nothing for you, so we anchor to reading instead. A fixed sequence, about 45 to 60 minutes before target bed:

1. Dim the lights and drop screen brightness (night mode).
2. Prep tomorrow (lay out training clothes, set the next action).
3. Read, fiction or non-fiction, until sleepy.

Same order every night so it becomes automatic.

## Screens and movies

You're on screens all day and watch movies at night, which is the hardest part of your wind-down. The realistic rule: in the last 45 minutes before bed, dim the screen hard, use night mode, and ideally switch the movie for the book. If you do watch, keep it dim and stop well before target bed.

## Dinner tie-in

Eating after 10pm feeds the late cycle. Your last meal pulls earlier as your wake time moves (this is run by the dinner stepper in the Diet system). Last meal well before bed.

## Caffeine

You don't use any, so it's not a factor. That's one fewer variable working against you, which makes this shift easier than it is for most people.

## The floor (bad night)

Even after a terrible night, wake at the target time. Do not sleep in to catch up, that drags the clock backward and undoes the step. If you're wrecked, a single nap of 20 minutes max in the early afternoon, never late.

## What the app runs

- Shows your current wake-time step and tonight's target bedtime.
- The wind-down checklist and the morning-light reminder.
- The check-in logs: actual wake time, bedtime, wind-down done, morning light done.
- Code computes wake-time consistency and decides when to advance the step.
- The coach advances the step when you're consistent, calls out drift, and ties low energy to short or late sleep using your real numbers.
